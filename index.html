Introducing Students to Biomedical Engineering

Young Medical Engineers is a nonprofit founded and run by high school students. We introduce students to one of the most important fields in modern medicine, and we support them with free tutoring in the courses that lead into it.

Who We Are

Young Medical Engineers was founded in 2026 by two students at the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy and is now led by a board of nine. Most students never encounter biomedical engineering until college, if they encounter it at all. We make the field visible through keynote speakers, design challenges, and hands-on demonstrations, and we support interested students with free tutoring in the science and math that lead into it.

More About Us

What We Do

Our programs serve two purposes: introducing students to biomedical engineering, and supporting those who decide to pursue it. A third effort helps students bring YME to their own schools.

Building Interest

Keynote speakers, talks with working engineers, design challenges with real prizes, and hands-on demonstrations in schools and libraries.

How We Build Interest

Supporting Students

Free, one-on-one tutoring in biology, chemistry, physics, and math, from students who are a few steps further along.
