Volunteer

Help a Student Find Their Path

A little of your time can open a door for a student. If you study or work in biomedical or medical engineering, or teach the sciences that lead into it, we'd love your help: tutoring a student, talking about your work, judging a design challenge, or helping run a demonstration.

You set your own availability, and we handle the scheduling and matching. Most of our volunteers come from university programs and local BMES chapters.

Ways You Can Help

  • Tutor a student one-on-one, online
  • Give a talk about your work and how you got there
  • Judge a design challenge
  • Help run a hands-on demonstration
