Opening the Door to Medical Engineering

We help students meet biomedical engineering, and we stay with the ones who decide to pursue it. You can even bring YME to your own school by starting a chapter.

Program 01

Building Interest

You can't aim for a career you've never heard of. We bring the field to students before college.

Our keynote events and smaller talks bring in people who work in biomedical engineering, including researchers, medical device founders, and leaders in the field. They speak with students in person and online about what the work involves, including its challenges, so students can meet people whose careers they might picture for themselves.

In our design challenges, students work on a current medical problem, such as making a treatment more affordable or improving recovery, and present their proposals to professionals in the field. We award prizes, and no lab or prior experience is required to take part. A strong idea is enough.

Our hands-on demonstrations travel to schools and libraries. Students might build a working stethoscope to hear their own heartbeat, or see a 3D printed prosthetic hand in action. We reuse the same equipment across events, so participation costs students nothing.

Program 02

Supporting Students

Once a student is hooked, we make sure a tough class never stands in their way.

Biomedical engineering builds on the sciences, so those are the subjects we tutor, one-on-one and at no cost: biology, chemistry, physics, math from algebra through calculus, anatomy and physiology, and AP and standardized-test science. Our tutors are students themselves, a few steps further along.

Program 03

Expanding Our Reach

Want YME at your school? Start a chapter, and we'll back you with a guide, volunteers, and funding.

Any student can start a chapter at their own school. We provide a guide for running it, connections to our volunteers, and a small startup grant. From there, the chapter is yours to lead.
