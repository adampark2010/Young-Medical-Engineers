We'd Love to Hear From You

Whether you're a student, parent, teacher, or professional in the field, reach out. For a classroom talk, a tutoring match, a partnership, or any other question, email us at youngmedicalengineers@gmail.com or find us on Instagram at @ym_engineers (opens in a new tab).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is tutoring free?
Yes. All of our programs are free, and they always will be.
Who are the tutors and speakers?
They're volunteer undergraduate and graduate students in biomedical and medical engineering, along with practicing engineers and keynote speakers who give their time to our talks and challenges.
What do you do?
We introduce students to biomedical engineering through keynote speakers, talks, design challenges, and demonstrations, and we support them with free tutoring in the sciences that lead into the field.
Where do you operate?
Tutoring and talks are online, so you can join from anywhere. In-person demonstrations and events take place wherever we have volunteers.
