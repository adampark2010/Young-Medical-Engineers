About Us

By Students, for Students

We are high school students. We started Young Medical Engineers in 2026 at the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy so more students could discover medical engineering before college, and get the help they need to pursue it.

Why We Do This

We want every student to discover medical engineering before college, and every student who wants to pursue it to have the support to get there.

Medical engineering is engineering in the service of people's health, and one of the broadest fields in modern medicine. It spans biomedical and biomechanical engineering, pharmaceutical engineering and drug discovery, medical imaging, prosthetics, and the devices clinicians rely on every day. Each is a specialized discipline in its own right, and few fields turn technical skill into direct, practical good, from a prosthetic that restores movement to a drug that changes the course of a disease.

Yet most students never hear of these fields until well into college, if they hear of them at all. By then, many of the courses and choices that lead into the field are already behind them. We believe students deserve to discover this work early, while there is still time to prepare for it.

So we do two things. We bring the field to students through keynote speakers, talks with working engineers, design challenges, and hands-on demonstrations. And we stand behind the students who get interested, with free tutoring in the science and math that lead into it.

Every program we offer is free, and it always will be. All you need to do is sign up.

What We Believe

  1. 01

    Free for Every Student

    No student should miss the chance to explore this field because of cost.

  2. 02

    Learning by Doing

    Students learn best with their hands busy. Whenever we can, we let students build and test projects themselves instead of only reading about them.

  3. 03

    Mentors Who Have Been There

    Our mentors remember what it was like. They are undergraduate and graduate students in biomedical engineering, along with practicing engineers, and many of them were in your shoes not long ago.

Where You'll Find Us

Our board members study at the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy, Dunlap High School, and Glenbrook South High School in Illinois, with country directors in India, Germany, and Korea.

Tutoring and talks happen online, so you can join from anywhere. Demonstrations and events happen in person wherever we have volunteers.

How It Works

You fill in the tutoring form, and a coordinator matches you with a volunteer tutor, usually within a few days. Teachers and librarians can request a talk or demonstration, and we bring the speakers and the equipment.

Volunteers set their own availability, and we handle the scheduling. It never costs a student anything.

A nonprofit founded and run by high school students, introducing others to biomedical engineering. Every program is free, and it always will be.
