Introducing Students to Biomedical Engineering

Young Medical Engineers is a nonprofit founded and run by high school students. We introduce students to one of the most important fields in modern medicine, and we support them with free tutoring in the courses that lead into it.

What We Do

Our programs serve two purposes: introducing students to biomedical engineering, and supporting those who decide to pursue it.

  1. 01

    Building Interest

    We make the field visible and engaging, so students can meet it before college.

    Learn More About Building Interest
  2. 02

    Supporting Students

    When a student becomes interested, we make sure coursework is not what holds them back.

    Learn More About Supporting Students
  3. 03

    Expanding Our Reach

    Students bring YME to their own schools, and we back them with a guide, volunteers, and funding.

    Learn More About Expanding Our Reach
