Executive Board

The Students Behind YME

It started with two students. At the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy, Sohum Kodilkar and Adam Park noticed that most students never meet biomedical engineering until college, if they meet it at all. They cared about engineering and medicine and wanted to give back, so in 2026 they founded Young Medical Engineers. Today a board of nine students runs free tutoring, talks, design challenges, and hands-on demonstrations for students exploring the field.
