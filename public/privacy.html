Privacy

What we collect through this site, what we do with it, and how to have it removed.

Who we are. Young Medical Engineers (“YME”, “we”) is a nonprofit run by high school students. This policy covers medengineers.org and the forms on it. Questions go to youngmedicalengineers@gmail.com.

What we collect. Only what you type into a form. For a tutoring request that is a student name, an email address, a grade level, a subject, and what you are working toward. For a volunteer application it is a name, email, institution or program, availability, how you would like to help, and the subjects you can help with. For a message it is a name, email, topic and the message itself. For a session sign-up, or to hear when new sessions open, it is a name, an email address, and an optional grade and note. Tutoring requests and session sign-ups also record that you confirmed you are 18 or older or have a parent or guardian’s permission. Please do not send medical, financial or other sensitive details through a form.

What we do not collect. We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and this site sets no cookies at all. After you sign up for a session, your first name and the session are kept in your browser’s session storage so the confirmation page can greet you; they stay on your device and are cleared when you close the tab.

What every website receives. Like any website, our web host (currently GitHub Pages; Cloudflare once we move there) receives basic technical information when your browser loads a page, such as your IP address and browser type, and may keep it briefly in server logs for security. That is covered by the host’s own privacy statement. We do not use those logs to identify anyone.

Content from other sites. A few pictures on our Articles page load directly from the news sources they belong to, such as the National Institutes of Health, the University of Southern California and Caltech, so those sites receive the same basic technical information when your browser loads them. Links to Instagram, PayPal and news stories take you to other sites with their own privacy policies.

How it reaches us. When our website runs with its database, form submissions and session sign-ups are saved in a private database that only board members can read, after signing in to our admin page; our email then gets only a short notice, with no personal details, through a third-party service, FormSubmit. If the database is not available, the form is emailed to us in full through FormSubmit instead, which passes the contents on and is subject to its own privacy terms. Either way, the information is seen only by the board members who arrange tutoring, volunteers and events.

What we use it for. Matching a student with a tutor, following up on a volunteer application, scheduling a talk or demonstration, replying to messages, sending the details of a session you signed up for, and, if you asked, telling you when new sessions open. That is all. We do not sell, rent or share personal information with anyone else, and we do not send marketing.

Students under 13. Our programs are open to middle school students, and some of them are under 13. If you are under 13, please ask a parent or guardian to fill in the form for you, or to email us on your behalf. A parent or guardian can contact us at any time to see what we hold about their child or to have it deleted, and we will act on that request.

Keeping, correcting and deleting it. We keep requests and sign-ups only while they are useful for running the program and answering you. Email youngmedicalengineers@gmail.com to see what we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, and we will do it and confirm when it is done.

Keeping it safe. Only board members can open the email account and database that hold your information, the admin page has its own sign-in, every page is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, and we do not copy your information anywhere else. No system is perfectly secure, so we collect as little as we can.

Donations. Donations are handled by PayPal on PayPal’s own site. We never see or store card or bank details. Our Donation Policy explains what PayPal shares with us.

Changes and contact. If this page changes we will update the date below. Questions about any of it go to youngmedicalengineers@gmail.com.
