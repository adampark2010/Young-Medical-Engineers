Contact Us

Whether you are a student, parent, teacher, or professional in the field, we would be glad to hear from you. For a classroom talk, a tutoring match, a partnership, or any other question, email us at youngmedicalengineers@gmail.com or reach us on Instagram at @ym_engineers (opens in a new tab).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is tutoring really free?
Yes. All of our programs are free, and that will not change.
Who are the tutors and speakers?
They are volunteer undergraduate and graduate students in biomedical and medical engineering, along with practicing engineers and keynote speakers who contribute to our talks and challenges.
What do you do?
We introduce students to biomedical engineering through keynote speakers, talks, design challenges, and demonstrations, and we support them with free tutoring in the sciences that lead into the field.
Where do you operate?
Tutoring and talks are held online and available anywhere. In-person demonstrations and events take place wherever we have volunteers.
