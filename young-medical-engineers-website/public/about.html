Who We Are

At the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy, Adam Park and Sohum Kodilkar noticed how few students their age had ever encountered medical engineering: the field behind prosthetics, imaging, drug discovery and the devices clinicians use every day. Most never hear of it until college, by which point the classes that lead into it are already behind them. Motivated by their passion for engineering and medicine and a desire to give back to their community, they founded Young Medical Engineers in 2026. Since then they have brought speakers, build events and free tutoring to students who would otherwise never have been offered them, including in India and Korea. Everything is free, and you only have to sign up.
