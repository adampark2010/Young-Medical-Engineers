Our Board

As students at the Illinois Mathematics and Science Academy, Sohum Kodilkar and Adam Park noticed that most students never encounter biomedical engineering until college, if they encounter it at all. Motivated by their interests in engineering and medicine and a desire to give back, they founded Young Medical Engineers in 2026. Since then, the organization has grown into a board of nine students who run free tutoring, talks, design challenges, and hands-on demonstrations for students exploring the field.
